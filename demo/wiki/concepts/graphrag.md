### Evidence notes

# GraphRAG GraphRAG is a retrieval architecture that extracts entities and relationships, builds graph-oriented indexes, and uses graph structure plus text evidence to improve retrieval and synthesis. Its center of gravity remains retrieval-time context construction. The graph index can help answer global and local questions by organizing entities, relationships, communities, and source text into queryable structures.
